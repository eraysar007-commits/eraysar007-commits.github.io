Frequently Asked Questions

What is AGENT XI and what is my goal?

AGENT XI is a football agency management game. As the owner of an agency you scout players and negotiate transfers and contracts with clubs, earning commissions to grow your agency. Your goal is to keep the agency healthy while raising its reputation and cash.

How does the game progress?

The game advances day by day. Each week has tasks and an action budget; when the budget runs out, you can do less that week.

Where should I start?

A new career opens with a 17-step tutorial. Completing it gives you a reward (including 2 Credits). The Help Center under Settings > Help explains the game's systems.

Is the game free?

Downloading the game is free. In-app purchases (Credit packs, Remove Ads, VIP subscription) are optional.

What is Gold, how do I earn it and what is it spent on?

Gold is the main currency. You earn it from agency commissions (for example transfer commissions). You spend it on things like the Scout fee (10,000 Gold), store items, and staff and scout salaries.

What are Credits? How are they different from Gold?

Credits are a second, premium currency, separate from Gold. You can convert Gold into at most 2 Credits per week (the rate depends on your agency level). Credits are scarce and are used for certain premium items. You can earn them from the tutorial, some in-game events and rewarded ads, or buy them in the store.

How do I make in-app purchases?

Use the Store screen in the game. Payment is charged to your Apple account; we never see your payment details.

How do I restore my purchases?

Use the “Restore Purchases” button on the Store screen (and the VIP screen). Purchases made with the same Apple account are restored.

What is VIP and what does it give me?

VIP is a weekly or monthly auto-renewing subscription. It gives conveniences such as ad-free play, bonuses to commission and mission rewards, extra weekly actions and extra save slots. The current list and the price in your local currency are shown on the in-game VIP screen.

How do I cancel my VIP subscription?

Cancel in your Apple account's subscription settings: Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the game does not cancel it.

What do I get for watching ads? How do I remove ads?

Optional rewarded ads can give you Credits, a free store refresh and a doubled mission reward; daily and weekly limits apply. Full-screen interstitial ads are rare and never appear at critical moments of the game. The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase or VIP removes ads.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, an “Ad privacy choices” row appears under Settings > Ads & Privacy. On iOS you can also use Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where is my save stored?

Only on this device. The game provides up to 8 save slots (5 open to everyone, 3 unlocked for new careers with VIP), and progress is written automatically to the selected slot as the game state changes.

What if my save is lost, or I move to a new phone?

There is no cloud backup and saves do not transfer to a new device. Deleting the app, resetting the device or clearing app data can remove your save permanently. We suggest keeping an important career in another slot too.

How do I delete all saves?

Go to Settings > Data and use “Reset save data”. A confirmation dialog appears; the action cannot be undone.

How do I change the language?

Under Settings > Language you can choose Turkish or English, or follow your device language.

What settings are available?

Large text, reduced animations, high contrast, sound and music options, the usage log (privacy) and save reset.
