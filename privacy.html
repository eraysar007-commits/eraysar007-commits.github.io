1. Overview

AGENT XI is a football management game. You run a football agency: you scout young players, sign representation contracts, and earn commissions in transfer negotiations. This policy explains what data the app handles and what we do with it.

You do not need an account. No registration data is sent to a server. Your game progress is stored on your device.

In-game data is not sent to our servers. Your game saves, settings and purchase ledger stay on your device. We do not share them with third parties.

Legal basis.

  • Providing the game and fulfilling purchases: establishment and performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Bug fixing and security: legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Personalized ads and tracking: your explicit consent (iOS tracking permission ATT; Google UMP form in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). You can withdraw consent at any time.

Collection method. Data is collected inside the app, by automated means, and through the Google AdMob and RevenueCat software development kits (SDKs).

International transfer. Purchase and advertising data is transferred to the following recipients in the United States: Google LLC (AdMob), RevenueCat Inc. and Apple Inc. The transfer falls under KVKK Art. 9. For personalized ads the basis is your explicit consent; other transfers are made to service providers so that purchase and advertising services can be provided.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details such as card numbers; the store handles them. RevenueCat is used to verify purchases and manage entitlements. RevenueCat links your purchases to an anonymous app user ID. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

4. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. There are no banner ads. There are two kinds of ads: rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen interstitial ads (never shown at critical moments of the game). Players with a VIP subscription or the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase see no ads.

  • Advertising identifier: AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads.
  • AdMob SDK data: In addition to ad performance, the AdMob SDK may process diagnostic and interaction data (for example ad impression and click events, app and device diagnostics).
  • iOS permission (ATT): The app asks for your permission before using the identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, the game works the same; ads are just not personalized.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: Before personal data is processed, the Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) consent form is shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > "Ad privacy choices" (the row appears in regions where consent is required).
  • AdMob's data processing is governed by Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Age requirement

AGENT XI is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us at eraygs001@hotmail.com and we will delete it from the device.

6. How to delete your data

  • Deleting game saves: Settings > Data > "Reset save data". This deletes all save slots (8). Your settings and purchase ledger are not deleted by this action.
  • Deleting the usage log: Settings > Privacy has a "Delete log" button; turning the log off also deletes the existing log.
  • Uninstalling the app: Removing the app deletes all local data on the device.
  • Purchase and advertising data: For data held by RevenueCat, AdMob and the store, use each company's deletion process. You can reset your advertising identifier in device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

7. Data security

Local saves are kept in the device's standard storage. We do not keep personal data on our own servers.

8. Your rights

  • to learn whether your personal data is processed;
  • to request information if it has been processed;
  • to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly;
  • to know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Turkey or abroad;
  • to request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate;
  • to request deletion or destruction under KVKK Art. 7;
  • to request that correction and deletion be notified to the third parties to whom data was transferred;
  • to object to a result against you arising from analysis of the data exclusively by automated systems;
  • to claim compensation if you suffer damage because the data was processed unlawfully.

EEA users also have rights under the GDPR to access, correct, delete, object to processing and data portability. Send your requests to eraygs001@hotmail.com.
